## Adaptation limits

The successful contrast is strong sans versus high-contrast italic, warm presence versus a clean stage, and stable labels versus developing mechanisms. Retain those relationships while adapting brand, story, aspect ratio, licensed fonts, permitted media count and audience reading time. Do not reuse the reference's cooking sequence, CTA wording or three-step structure indiscriminately. A tutorial screen requires fitting its readable interface rather than applying a portrait crop.
