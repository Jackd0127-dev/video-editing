# Reference: "5 million views in 35 days" motion reel (Jack's benchmark for interactivity)

Source: instagram.com/reel/De9RuthIkyL (saved at `references/motion-reels/ig-De9RuthIkyL.mp4`, 39 s). Jack: "this is what
I meant by more interactive — constantly eye-catching, good for a viewer, looks great. Follow its instructions."

## Its rules (spoken in the reel — follow them)
1. **Hook people in the first second.**
2. **Time the voice.**
3. **Land every picture exactly on its word.**
4. **Built to be watched to the end: hooked in the first second, moving until the last frame.**

## What it does on screen (measured: ~78 distinct visual states in 39 s ≈ one every 0.5 s)
- **Every phrase is a prop, not a label.** 5 million → a real analytics card counting 4,994,055 → 5,000,000 → "5M"; 35 days →
  a tear-off calendar flipping 1 → 35; "never filmed a frame" → a film camera that gets a big X; "on my Mac" → a laptop
  with flying screenshots and a price-tag sticker "MY MAC"; "the secret" → a vault door that opens on the Claude logo,
  then a stamp; "one folder" → a folder under a spotlight; "221 files" → a tile grid counting up; "the rules" → a rule
  book; "hook in 1 second" → a stopwatch card; "time the voice" → a waveform card; "picture on its word" → a storyboard
  that ticks; "why do they blow up" → a retention graph with a rocket line; "comment SKILL" → the real Instagram comment
  sheet filling with comments.
- **Props are illustrated/3D-looking objects** (camera, vault, laptop, folder, stopwatch, pencil, phones) with real
  screenshots inside screens — never flat pills or generic chips.
- **Numbers always move** (count-ups, flips, progress bars, 1-2-3 trackers that persist at the bottom of the frame).
- **Negation is physical**: a red X drawn over the object, a stamp ("NOT IT"), a strike through the word.
- **Type**: kinetic caption top-left, word by word, the key word swapped into a serif italic accent; big heavy
  condensed display words (ISN'T, WHY?, YOU CAN DO THIS.) slam in under the prop.
- **Transitions**: halftone-dot wipes and quick pushes between props; nothing holds still.
- **Persistent structure**: a numbered tracker (1 2 3) shows where you are in the list.

## How to apply in the 30 Day AI Series
- Plan a **prop per phrase**: for each line, name the object that *is* the idea (price → price tag on a receipt / app
  store card; "every day" → calendar; "phone" → a real phone with the real screen; "developers" → a dev at a desk /
  code editor; "subscription" → the real App Store subscription sheet getting cancelled).
- Count states: aim for a new visual state every ~0.5–1 s; never a pill-only beat.
- Real screens inside props (his screenshots, App Store, Instagram comments) beat drawn ones.
- Keep the series palette (ink on paper, real brand colours in logos/screens) — the reference's red accent is its own.
