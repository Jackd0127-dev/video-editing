# Rules

## This style
- **Captions**: one word at a time (pair two short words only when spoken together), white Montserrat ExtraBold ALL CAPS, on the chest just below the chin. Hard switch on each word — no animation, no colour, and never two captions on screen at once.
- **Hook**: open on his face with a quick zoom-out slam, plus one designed overlay. A pile of comment cards was rejected ("very bad"); a glass banner title with the little pixel Claude character was approved.
- **Overlays are liquid glass**: translucent white gradient, backdrop blur + saturation, bright 1.5 px rim, inner top highlight, soft shadow, a sheen sweep. Rounded corners must be anti-aliased (see style-system). Content centred with **equal padding left and right**.
- **Entrances are gentle**: ~0.8–0.9 s fade + small drop + blur-off, `power3.out`. No fast bouncy scale-ins for big boxes.
- **UI mock-ups feel real**: natural uneven typing with a caret, send button lights when ready, a cursor glides in and clicks (press + ripple + click sound), the result animates out smoothly.
- **No colour grade, no cut-outs, no pages** — his natural footage stays full-frame the whole time.
- Punch-ins alternate 1.0 / 1.15 at every cut and phrase break; no transitions besides hard cuts.

## Shared with every edit
- Cut every dead gap, including inside sentences, and sniffs/breaths at edges.
- Real brand marks only; never redraw logos.
- Masked or hidden text never peeks early; check by stepping frames, not single snapshots.
- Captions never cover his face.
