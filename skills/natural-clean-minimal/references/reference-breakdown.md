# Theme 2 — reference breakdown

Source: `ref.mp4` (TikTok download, 38.9 s, 576×1024 @29.97). "Day 19 of teaching you how to create content", a single-tip talking head.

## Overall
- Minimal, fast, utilitarian. The footage carries almost everything; graphics only appear when they clarify a fact.
- Talking speed ~219 words/min, so the edit is tight jump cuts with no pauses.
- One locked-off camera: bright, airy room, natural daylight, black tee. The only grade is a clean, slightly cool, high-key look.

## Captions (the main device)
- One word at a time, occasionally two ("ON CAPCUT", "IF YOU", "WITH A").
- White, bold geometric sans, **ALL CAPS**, small (~3.5 % of frame height), with a faint shadow.
- Centred, on the chest, at ~55–60 % of frame height. That's below the face and steady, never moving.
- Hard switch on each word: no animation, no highlight, no colour. The word just changes.

## Title card
- "DAY 19" (bold caps, ~2× caption size) with a small caps subtitle "TEACHING YOU HOW TO CREATE CONTENT" under it.
- Same chest position as the captions. Shown for ~1.5 s, after the hook line.

## Graphics
- **Platform logos**: Instagram, YouTube Shorts and TikTok icons pop in one by one, left to right, as each is named. They sit in a row just above the caption, and stay while the sentence continues.
- **Screen recording**: a full-height phone recording (CapCut) is inset over the footage. It keeps the phone's own UI and the face behind it is hidden. A **hand-drawn white circle** scribbles around the setting he's pointing at ("top right"). The export progress counts up live (23 % → 99 % → Ready to share) while he keeps talking. The captions continue over it.

## Effects
- **Pixelation as the hook and outro.** The video opens heavily pixelated and resolves to sharp over ~2.5 s, timed to "you should never upload your videos in 4K". It re-pixelates on the last word ("why…") so it loops. The effect *is* the topic (compression / quality).
- Punch-ins between jump cuts: scale alternates roughly 100 % → 115 %.
- No transitions besides hard cuts and the pixel resolve.

## Sound
- Voice-forward, −19 LUFS, very compressed (LRA 2.4).
- Quiet plucked-guitar/acoustic music bed.
- No obvious SFX.

## Rhythm
- Hook (0–2.5 s, pixel resolve) → series title (2.5–5.5 s) → fact with logos (6–11 s) → explanation, captions only (11–33 s) → screen-recording proof with circle annotation (33–38 s) → re-pixelate outro.
