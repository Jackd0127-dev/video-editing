An audio tutorial could show available processors, isolate one signal path, then demonstrate the audible result. A product film could trace energy or material through one mechanism.

Transfer a **moving category strip** only when breadth matters and individual labels are secondary. Use real, licensed/approved marks, a clean textual list, or neutral symbols as appropriate. Avoid implying partnerships or endorsements.
