## What to transfer

Transfer **evidence → marked pattern → consequence → alternative**. A different editorial film could compare three product behaviours, annotate one failure, then show a redesigned process using clean footage and diagrams. The persuasive structure does not require a green background, tape, serif/italic pairing, or a rejection stamp.

Transfer **visible feedback** to any action where a changed state matters. In a craft film, a seam is secured; in an instrument film, a tone stabilises; in a software demo, a saved item appears. Pick the expression suited to the brief.

Do not transfer the apparent source warning into a blanket ban on reusable design systems. Consistency can be appropriate; blindly inherited appearance is the failure mode. Check that a new film has its own reasons for composition, typography, motion, material, and pacing.
