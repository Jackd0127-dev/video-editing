## Reverse-engineered motion and text

**Observed:** The second numbered note enters with scale/position change rather than requiring a new full-screen scene. Repeated screenshot cards form a fan after the dominant screenshot. The receipt's content and highlights develop over time. Consecutive timing crops near 34s show a chip scaling up quickly, reaching a larger size, then reducing slightly. This supports a brief overshoot-like arrival; it does not reveal the exact easing function.

**Inferred construction:** Build each numbered note as a grouped paper shape, title, and descriptor. Animate the second independently, keep both steady during the explanation, and remove the obsolete comparison state after its job is complete. For the chips, use a rounded shape with border and text, then animate a controlled scale arrival around an appropriate anchor. Match category timing to speech rather than using identical delays.

Build the screenshot fan with one dominant source and secondary grouped cards. Animate their transforms as complete objects; annotations belong to the relevant source group. Draw the circle after the source settles. For the receipt, use aligned repeated rows and separate background/highlight layers.

## Transfer and alternatives

Transfer **familiar anchor → unfamiliar counterpart → meaningful difference** to any explanation that needs a comparison. A product demo could show two behaviours in matched footage; a scientific film could compare one controlled variable; a calm corporate piece could use two clean labels with no texture.

Transfer **repetition → summary** when small actions form a larger consequence. Use an actual tally or simplified illustrative sequence, not fabricated financial proof. Change the metaphor to pulses, transactions in a table, footsteps, or progress ticks when paper does not fit.

Keep this method distinct from R01's creative expansion: its priority is comparison and mechanism. Quality checks: equal comparison scale, legible key labels, accurate categories, adequate holds, and consistent annotation targets. Do not add every receipt or chip merely because the source used one.
