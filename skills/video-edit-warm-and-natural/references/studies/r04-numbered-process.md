## Recreate and adapt

Use **consistent teaching grammar, changing mechanism** for a list whose ideas belong together. A cooking tutorial can repeat preparation/problem → technique → result. An engineering explainer can repeat component → stress → consequence → correction. Make the chapter structure suited to the amount of information; five steps are not a universal requirement.

Use **literal text above, visual consequence below** only when the two add distinct value. A calmer version could use a single brief instruction and one clear animation, without a terminal skin. A more tactile film could demonstrate real objects and reserve type for section labels.
