## Transfer without copying

Use **unit → field → active process → isolated exception** for topics where scale matters. A factory documentary might move from one part to a production line, then isolate a fault. A data film might introduce one record, expand to a dataset, trace processing, then highlight an outlier. A music visual can reveal one rhythmic voice, multiply it into an ensemble, and return to a solo.

Change material and tempo to fit the brief. A technical enterprise film might use aligned wireframes and precise pauses. A cinematic film might show the system through real footage and spatial sound. A playful piece might keep hand-drawn motion but invent a new metaphor.

Checks: understandable unit before multiplication, stable recognition at small scale, deliberate stagger direction, coherent motion hierarchy, meaningful reading holds, and an exception highlighted for a reason. Do not use a mascot swarm or task grid when a single arrow explains the relationship better.
